L'aventure
UMEO

The Umeo adventure was born from a crazy bet: to create a consulting company where personal ambitions and well-being meet. To put it simply, Umeo is a company that resembles us and places at the top of its concerns the redefinition of the relationship between candidates and consulting companies (and that's crazy 💪!).

The team currently operates in two major cities: Paris, the City of Lights, and Lyon, famous for its Festival of Lights. The place to be, to sparkle and shine! 💥

Our Lyon office is located near Jean Macé Square (around 15 meters from the metro for the sporty ones) and our Paris premises, on Rue de Monceau, 500 meters from the park (nothing better for a good tan). 😎

Our wish ?

Bring the projects of our candidates, consultants and clients to life by combining harmony and mutual assistance (and all this with a smile, please!).

Your satisfaction is our top priority. To meet your needs, our team will deploy its expertise and goodwill all while trying to exceed your expectations. (And yeah, just that 🔥!).

All this to support health industries: pharmaceutical companies and medical device manufacturers.
