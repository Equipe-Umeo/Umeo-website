UMEO
What is it?

It’s everyone’s opportunity to contribute to impactful projects that are dear to them : social and environmental works. Thanks to funding from Umeo, the foundation benefits from an annual budget allowing it to carry out impactful actions. This gives meaning to the company and to the jobs for all the people who are members of the Umeo project.

Umeo is also setting up a skills sponsorship : Two weeks of inter-contract? And why not take advantage of this to set up food distribution for the most deprived? Or give your time to the association near you?

During an inter-contract at Umeo, you have the opportunity to contribute to projects that have an impact on you, society and the environment. Nice, right?

As you see, we want to give meaning to the company and that everyone can at their own level. This is why Umeo decided to give its employees the opportunity to get involved in a cause that is close to their hearts. If you don't have one that comes to mind, don't worry, we will direct you to one of our partner associations.

The foundation is participative and democratic in its operation. Its aim is to enable everyone to have an impact, because the company also has its role to play in the challenges to come.
