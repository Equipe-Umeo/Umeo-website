Qualification - Validation

Whether as part of a revamping project or a new equipment/processes , our teams support you at all stages of the V-cycle:

  • Writing a Validation Master Plan

  • Writing the URS (User Requirement Specifications)

  • Definition of CQA (Critical Quality Attributes and CPP (Critical Process Parameters)

  • Risk Analysis

  • FAT/ SAT

  • Writing IQ/QO/QP protocols and process validation

  • Planning, organizing and monitoring test executions

  • Writing reports

  • Definition of periodic requalification/revalidation expectations

  • Setting Periodic Review Expectations

  • Audit of your qualification/validation (Q/V) processes in place
