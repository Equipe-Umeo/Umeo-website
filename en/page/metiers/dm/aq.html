Qualification - Validation

Whether as part of a revamping project or new equipment/processes , our teams support you at all stages of the V-cycle:

  • Writing of a validation master plan

  • Writing user requirements (URS: User Requirement Specifications)

  • Risk Analysis

  • FAT/ SAT

  • Writing QC/IQ/OQ/PQ protocols and process validation

  • Planning, organizing and monitoring test executions

  • Writing reports

  • Definition of periodic requalification/revalidation expectations

  • Audit of your current qualification/validation (Q/V) processes

  • Validation of special processes: Cleaning validation, Sterilization validation (H202 sterilization, Steam, Ethylene oxide, Dry heat, Gamma irradiation), Cleanroom qualification, Validation of computerized and automated systems
