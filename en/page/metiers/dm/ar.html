Regulatory Affairs - Medical Devices

Expertise

Our consultants benefit from rigorous training and extensive experience allowing them to support you in your various issues:

  • Definition of the approval strategy: determination of the class of your medical device or In Vitro medical device

  • Writing and updating your technical files in compliance with the MDR (2017/745) and the IVDR (2017/746)

  • Support for responses from your notified body

  • Regulatory and standards monitoring

  • Risk management according to ISO 14971: 2019

  • Design file compliant with ISO 13485 : 2016

  • Audit, writing and updating of your clinical evaluation reports (CER) according to MEDDEV 2.7/1 rev 4

Finally, our specialized teams will be able to support you on the specificities of marketing for export, particularly in the USA, Japan and Brazil

Guidelines and Standards

Our interventions comply with current regulations and standards :

  • Medical Device Regulation : 2017/745

  • In Vitro Diagnostic Regulation : 2017/746

  • ISO 13485 : 2016 : Quality and conformity

  • ISO 14971 :2019 : risk management

  • MEDDEV 2.7/1 rev 4

  • IEC 62366-1: usability

  • IEC 62304 : Software life cycle processes

  • Suite IEC 60601-1 : Medical electrical equipment

  • GMP, Part I, Part II, Part III, Annex 11 : Computerised Systems, Annex 15 : Qualification and Validation
